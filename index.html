OPTIONAL · BLUETOOTH LIVE VIEW

See your heart rate as it happens.

Your Forerunner 255 Music can broadcast heart rate directly to this dashboard in Chrome on your Mac. If this app’s browser does not offer Bluetooth, open this same local dashboard address in Chrome.

Start broadcasting on your watch

  1. Hold UP to open the watch menu.
  2. Select Health & Wellness → Wrist Heart Rate → Broadcast Heart Rate.
  3. Press START, then choose Connect watch in the dashboard and select your watch.

Keep broadcasting active while you use the live view. This connects to the heart rate broadcast and does not change watch settings.

A PLACE FOR YOUR OTHER DATA

Import from Apple Health

On your iPhone, open Health → Summary → your profile picture → Export All Health Data. Save the export to your computer, then choose the ZIP file or export.xml below.

Files stay on this computer. Maximum file size: 150 MB.

SET UP YOUR WYZE SCALE

Let Apple Health bring it together.

  1. Open your scale in the Wyze app.
  2. Go to Settings → Authorize 3rd-Party Apps.
  3. Select Apple Health → Authorize and allow the measurements you want to share.

Available measurements depend on what Wyze writes to Apple Health. Imported sources remain separate to avoid double counting.

YOUR TIME OUTSIDE

Import an AllTrails hike

On the AllTrails website, go to Profile → Activities, select a recorded activity, then choose … → Download route → GPX. Import that file here.

Maximum 16 MB. Recorded timestamps are required; planned routes are not activity history. GPX duration is elapsed time and includes pauses.

Garmin and AllTrails recordings stay separate. Choose a source in Workouts to avoid counting the same hike twice.

Import a CSV from another monitor

Use a CSV with columns date,metric,value,unit,source. Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Supported metrics: weightKg, systolic, diastolic, glucoseMgDl, spo2, and temperatureC.

This is a standard import format. A device’s own export may need to be converted first.
